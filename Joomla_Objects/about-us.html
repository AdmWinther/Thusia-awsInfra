👩‍💻 About Us

We’re a passionate collective of self-taught web developers driven by curiosity, creativity, and the joy of building. What started as a simple idea—to learn new skills by doing—has grown into a vibrant initiative where experimentation meets purpose.

At our core, we believe that learning is best done hands-on, and that knowledge becomes truly valuable when shared. Our mission is to create useful web applications that benefit the community, and to make those projects freely accessible wherever possible (as far as our budget allows!).

We're not a conventional development studio—we're a welcoming space for volunteers who thrive on challenge, collaboration, and discovery. If you’re someone who loves learning by doing, enjoys coding for a cause, and wants to be part of a dynamic, proactive team, we’d be thrilled to have you onboard.

If you are interested to join us, please send us an email. You can find our contact info in "Contact us" page.

Join us in shaping ideas into reality. Let’s learn, build, and grow—together.
